# Sprint 3 Plan

## Sprint Goal

Introduce faction management and improve NPC organization while beginning session tracking capabilities.

This sprint focuses on enabling Dungeon Masters to group, filter, and manage NPCs more effectively, while laying the groundwork for tracking campaign sessions.

---

## Sprint Scope

Sprint 3 centers on delivering a complete faction workflow and improving NPC usability through filtering and sorting.

Additionally, the sprint introduces the initial implementation of session logs to begin capturing campaign progression.

---

## Committed User Stories

- Create Faction (3)  
- Assign NPC to Faction (3)  
- View Faction Members (2)  
- Filter NPCs by Faction (3)  
- Sort NPC List (2)  
- Create Session Log (5)  

**Total Commitment:** 18 Story Points

---

## Expected Outcome

By the end of Sprint 3 the application should support:

- Creating faction records  
- Assigning NPCs to one or more factions  
- Viewing all NPCs within a faction  
- Filtering NPC lists by faction  
- Sorting NPC lists for improved usability  
- Creating session logs to track campaign events  

These capabilities significantly improve organization within LoreSmith and provide the first step toward tracking narrative progression across sessions.

---

## Notes

Sprint 3 builds on the entity expansion introduced in Sprint 2 by adding relationships between NPCs and factions.

Session functionality is intentionally introduced at a basic level, with more advanced features such as tagging NPCs in sessions and searching sessions deferred to future iterations.

The team is planning conservatively at **18 story points** to maintain consistency with prior sprint velocity and allow buffer for integration and review improvements identified during the Sprint 2 retrospective.
